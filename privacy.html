Privacy policy · BIZNES.EXE BETA

Updated: 2026-10-26.

  • The game runs in your browser. There are no accounts, sign-in, chat or real-money purchases.
  • We do not collect personal data. No analytics, ads, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • Progress and settings are stored only on this device (localStorage, bx- keys). They are never sent to a server. A damaged save is copied to bx-save-backup. Local results are kept in bx-records and the age choice (<18 / 18+) in bx-age. Under 18 the patron links are inactive.
  • Files for offline play are kept in the browser cache (service worker).
  • A new game replaces the save. Clearing site data in the browser deletes the save and settings.
  • The game is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical logs including IP addresses. See: GitHub General Privacy Statement (docs.github.com).
  • This page has no external links. The game screen links to patron websites; external sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Contact: Issues in the github.com/sfaragdas/BIZNES.EXE repository.
