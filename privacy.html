Privacy policy · BIZNES.EXE 2.0 retro

Updated: 2026-10-25.

  • The game runs in your browser. There are no accounts, sign-in, chat or real-money purchases.
  • We do not collect personal data. No analytics, ads, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • Progress, records, settings and the age declaration are stored only on this device (localStorage, bx2r- keys). They are never sent to a server.
  • Files for offline play are kept in the browser cache (service worker).
  • Clearing site data in the browser deletes the save. In the game: Settings → Delete saved game.
  • The game is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical logs including IP addresses. See: GitHub General Privacy Statement (docs.github.com).
  • This page has no external links. Patron links work only after the 18+ declaration. External sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Contact: Issues in the github.com/sfaragdas/BIZNES.EXE repository.
